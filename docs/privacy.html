Privacy

Ocadox is built to keep your work on your computer. It has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send your prompts, files, chats or voice anywhere.

Last updated October 9, 2026.

In short: models run on your machine through Ollama, speech is turned into text on your machine with Whisper, and your chats, bots and settings are files in your user folder. Ocadox only goes online for the specific things listed below, most of which you start yourself.

What stays on your computer

  • Your chats, bots and their transcripts, routines, settings and checkpoints.
  • Everything the agent reads or changes in the project folder you give it.
  • Your voice: the microphone is recorded and transcribed locally, and the recording is not kept.
  • Energy and temperature history, and crash logs.

Your other computers

With Ocadox Pro, a laptop can use an always-on PC's bots. The two talk directly (over your network or Tailscale), encrypted, after you pair them with a one-time code. Nothing passes through any server of ours; there isn't one.

Buying Ocadox Pro

Checkout is handled by Gumroad, which processes your payment and email under Gumroad's privacy policy. Ocadox itself only stores your license key, on your computer.

Bug reports

"Send feedback" copies details (Ocadox version, system, GPU, and a crash report if there is one) to your clipboard and opens a GitHub issue. Nothing is sent unless you paste it and submit it yourself, so you can read or trim it first.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and has no analytics. GitHub may keep server logs, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy statement.
