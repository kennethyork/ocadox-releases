What's new

Every release, newest first. Ocadox updates itself: you get each one from inside the app. Download the latest.

0.7.4 October 12, 2026

  • Long video to Shorts (Tools > Long video to Shorts, or Shorts under an MP4): Whisper writes down what's said, your local model picks the moments that work on their own, and each one is made vertical (9:16) with big word-by-word captions and its title. Tick, rename or nudge them first. Saved beside the video, all on this PC.
  • The agent and bots can make Shorts too (video_shorts): they read the transcript, pick the moments and save them in the project.

0.7.3 October 12, 2026

  • Board (Tools > Board): a Trello-style board for each project, kept on this PC. Columns, cards with notes, labels, due dates and checklists, drag and drop. Do it hands a card to the agent, which ticks the checklist and moves it to Done.
  • Bots can work through a board column one card at a time (Pro): bot routines > When a card is in a board column.
  • Local servers like Ollama: their replies get the same quiet pacing; LM Studio's models are in the model library (fit labels, Load, Unload, Download) and in the In VRAM panel, and unload when idle.
  • Model library > Cloud: OpenRouter's models with prices, and your Codex, picked the same way as local ones.

0.7.2 October 12, 2026

  • Local servers: use models from LM Studio, llama.cpp, vLLM, Jan, KoboldCpp or text-generation-webui, on this PC or your network. Settings > Local servers > Find servers. They're local models: no CLOUD tag, and the fan guard and power cap apply.

0.7.1 October 12, 2026

  • Edit by transcript (Tools > Edit by transcript, or Transcript under a video): Whisper writes down every word on this PC; click words to cut them, remove filler words and long pauses in one click, add captions that follow the speech, and turn on studio sound.
  • Overdub: in Fix words, pick what you said and type what you meant; it's re-spoken in your saved voice and put in its place, with the picture held to fit.
  • Edit video: cut out parts from the middle, and add text that shows between chosen times.

0.7.0 October 12, 2026

  • Use your Codex: have a ChatGPT Plus or Pro plan? Sign in with ChatGPT in Settings > Cloud models and use OpenAI's Codex as a model, on your plan's limits. Needs Codex installed (npm install -g @openai/codex). In agent mode it works in the chat's folder and asks through Ocadox before anything risky.
  • Record Ocadox: Tools > Record Ocadox records this window as a video, and Record a demo records while the agent does a task, stopping when it's done. Then Edit it or add a voice-over.

0.6.9 October 12, 2026

  • Ocadox for Mac (Apple Silicon): the .dmg on the download page. Until it's signed for Mac, open it the first time with right-click > Open.
  • Git panel (Tools > Git): see your changes and diffs, commit with a message your local model writes, push, pull, switch branches, clone, and open GitHub pull requests.
  • A calmer top bar: video, picture editing, voice-overs, recording, characters, the Poster and Git are now in one Tools menu.

0.6.8 October 12, 2026

  • Sign in with OpenRouter: in Settings > Cloud models, press the button and approve in your browser; no key to copy. Optional and off until you choose it.
  • Pick from OpenRouter's 450+ models, with prices: Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek and more. Agent-ready shows the ones that can use the agent's tools.

0.6.7 October 12, 2026

  • Longer videos: choose 2 seconds to 2 minutes. Long videos are made as 5-second parts, each starting where the last ended, and saved as an MP4. Write one line per scene for a storyboard.
  • Voice-overs: pick any video, type a script or have your local model draft one, and Ocadox speaks it in your saved voice and adds it to the video, with captions if you like.
  • Poster: Sign in with Mastodon in your browser, no token to copy, and a button that opens the right page for every other platform.
  • The browser tool now asks before opening, clicking or typing on a website outside your PC.
  • Edit pictures by asking ("make it night", "change the sign to SALE") and upscale them 4x: press Edit or Upscale under any picture. Set up once in Settings > Image creation > Picture editing.
  • Quick video edits: press Edit under a video and describe it ("vertical for TikTok, cut the first 2 seconds, text at the top"). Your local model fills in the trim, shape, music and text.
  • Record a website as a video: a smooth scroll through any page, desktop or phone sized (command palette > Record a website).
  • Characters, for AI influencers: a face that stays the same in new photos, a personality, a voice, videos and posts with an AI label. Free has 1 character, Pro 5.
  • Bots can now make and edit videos, voice-overs, recordings and pictures, and post them. Videos post to Mastodon, Discord and Bluesky.
  • Images and videos now check how much video memory is free, so they run beside other programs that use the graphics card.
  • Terms, refunds and open-source licences, linked from Settings > Pro.
  • Android app: adding a PC no longer closes the app after you allow the camera. Scanning the code now uses Google's scanner, so Ocadox doesn't need camera access at all.

0.6.6 October 11, 2026

  • Make videos on this PC: /video_create a cat walking through autumn leaves, or Animate under any generated image (Wan 2.2, set up once in Settings > Image creation). Needs a graphics card.
  • Speech in your voice: add a short sample of your voice in Settings, then /speak and what to say (Qwen3-TTS, on this PC). Only voices that are yours or that you have permission to use.
  • The Poster: write, schedule and publish posts to Bluesky, Mastodon, Discord and Slack (free: 1 brand, 20 posts a month), plus X and Reddit with Pro (5 brands, 100 posts a month). Bots can draft posts for your OK, and with Pro post on their own where you allow it.

0.6.5 October 11, 2026

  • Your quiet week: how long Ocadox worked in the last 7 days and how quiet your PC stayed, as a card you can share (in the thermals panel, and offered once a week).
  • Bots that react: a routine can now run when files change in the bot's folder (or only *.py and the like), or after a new commit. A bot's own edits never set it off.
  • Share bots as files: Share as a file in the bot editor, From a file when making one. No folders or chats go in the file, and shared routines start switched off.

0.6.4 October 11, 2026

  • Helpers: the agent can hand a question to a helper with a fresh, empty context, which keeps long tasks on track with local models. One at a time, on the same model, under the same fan guard: the fans stay calm.
  • Before finishing, the agent checks its own changes with the project's check (cargo check, tsc, go build, Python), or a Check: line in AGENTS.md, and fixes what it finds.
  • Hooks: an After edit: line in AGENTS.md or OCADOX.md (say, npx prettier --write {file}) runs after every edit.
  • A browser for the agent: it can open your web app (in Chrome or Edge, hidden), click, type, and read the page and its console errors.
  • From a terminal: ocadox run "fix the failing tests" --folder path.
  • Optional cloud models with your own key (OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic and others), off until you add one in Settings, marked CLOUD.

0.6.3 October 11, 2026

  • Send feedback now includes a problem report (system, graphics, memory, CPU mode, Ollama and the loaded models, the last errors), with no names, folders or chats. You see it before sending.
  • PCs without a graphics card: the model library says which models fit this PC's memory and which would freeze it, and first-run setup suggests a small one.
  • Linux: a steadier window on some graphics drivers.
  • Android app: notifications when a bot finishes or needs your OK even with the app closed (a quiet notification keeps it watching; switch it off on the computers screen), Update installs a new version from inside the app, and images from the full app can be saved to the phone.

0.6.2 October 11, 2026

  • PCs without a graphics card no longer freeze while a model answers: one core is left for the desktop by default, the reply is redrawn a few times a second instead of on every word, and a model too big for the free memory is refused with a message instead of loading.
  • The whole desktop app on your phone: on the PC, turn on Full control for your phone (Settings > Other computers) and the Android app opens the full Ocadox, running on the PC. Folders are chosen on the phone from the PC's own.
  • Android app: a PC that can't be reached says so, with the reason and Try again, instead of an empty list. Lists and chats show Loading, and a message you send appears at once.

0.6.1 October 10, 2026

  • Image creation, built in: Settings > Image creation downloads an image engine and the Z-Image Turbo model once (6.7 GB, every file checked). Then type /image_create and what you want. Works on NVIDIA, AMD and Intel graphics, or slowly on the processor, with the fan guard and power cap.

0.6.0 October 10, 2026

  • CPU mode, for PCs without a graphics card that can hold a model (office desktops, built-in Intel or AMD graphics). It turns on by itself when Ollama runs the model on the processor: a smaller context window and no hidden thinking, so replies start sooner.
  • Quiet on the processor too: a processor limit (all cores, all but one, or half), and the fan guard and the Silent and Balanced profiles watch the processor's temperature.
  • Processor power while generating: Normal, Reduced or Low, then put back. Windows turns turbo boost off (Low also holds the processor to 70%) with no administrator rights. Linux lowers the processor's own power limit to about 60% or 40%, or exact watts, after a one-time Allow.
  • Windows: the processor's temperature on more PCs, including without administrator rights.
  • Linux: installs from the .deb or .rpm now update themselves from inside the app, like Windows and the AppImage.

0.5.5 October 10, 2026

  • Phone app: much less lag while a bot or chat is replying, especially when the PC is slower. The reply's progress is sent a few times a second instead of on every word, and the phone redraws once per frame.

0.5.4 October 10, 2026

  • Small screens: clearer Menu and temperature buttons at the top for the sidebar and the thermals, and the window can now be as small as 480 by 400.

0.5.3 October 10, 2026

  • Smaller screens and windows: the panels make room, then the thermals and the sidebar slide in from buttons at the top. Works with display scaling on Windows and Linux.

0.5.2 October 10, 2026

  • Try Pro free for 7 days: Settings > Ocadox Pro. No key or card needed.
  • AMD and Intel graphics: the fan guard and the GPU panel now work with them too (Windows and Linux), not just NVIDIA.
  • Android: the app tells you when a new version is out.
  • Ollama cloud models are marked CLOUD; Ocadox explains once what that means before you use one, and Settings > Ollama server can hide them.

0.5.1 October 10, 2026

  • Phones: the pairing QR code now opens the Ocadox Android app instead of the browser. No app? A button shows the browser code.
  • Phones: a Chats tab next to Bots. Read your PC's chats, or start a quick chat with its model (no tools); it's saved in the PC's chat list.
  • Phones: turn a bot's routines on or off, or run one now.

0.5.0 October 10, 2026

  • Bot templates: start a new bot as a test watcher, code reviewer, dependency checker, weekly summary or folder tidier.
  • Pro: your bots on your phone. Get the Ocadox Android app from the download page, then Settings > Other computers > Pair a computer or phone, and scan the QR code. Add all your Windows and Linux PCs; chat, stop and approve from the phone.
  • When a bot finishes, the notification shows the start of its answer. Routines can also save each result as a file.
  • Ocadox starts Ollama by itself when it isn't running, so routines work right after a restart.

0.4.1 October 9, 2026

  • Models: drag the model cards up and down to put them in the order you like. The order is remembered.

0.4.0 October 9, 2026

  • Voice: click the microphone by the message box and talk. Your words are written in for you, on this computer, with Whisper.
  • Settings > Voice: choose the speech model (Small by default) and language.
  • Pro: the agent can transcribe audio and video files in your project, with timestamps.

0.3.1 October 9, 2026

  • Bots stay free. Routines, Always on and Other computers are now part of Ocadox Pro.

0.3.0 October 9, 2026

  • Bots: agents you come back to, each with its own avatar, chat, folder, model and instructions. Their avatars show what they're doing.
  • Bots keep working, and keep their results, with the window closed.
  • Routines: schedule a bot (daily, weekdays, weekly or hourly). It can even wake the PC from sleep.
  • Settings > Always on: start Ocadox with Windows, quietly in the tray.
  • Settings > Other computers: use an always-on PC's bots from your laptop, securely paired.

0.2.1 October 8, 2026

0.2.0 October 8, 2026

  • Project instructions now live in OCADOX.md (/init writes one for you).

0.1.1 October 8, 2026

0.1.0 October 8, 2026

  • Ctrl+K command palette, Ctrl+N new chat, Esc to stop, and a light theme.
  • /image_create makes images on this PC: built in (set up once in Settings), or with your own Forge server.
