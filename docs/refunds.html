Try it first: the app has a free 7-day Pro trial, so you can check it works on your computer before you pay.

How to get a refund

  1. Within 30 days of buying, email kenneth.york@kennethyork.com from the address you bought with, or reply to your Gumroad receipt.

What happens to your licence

A refund deactivates the licence key. Ocadox notices the next time it checks the key (about once a month) and goes back to the free version. Your chats, bots, files and settings stay as they are.

After 30 days

Purchases older than 30 days aren't normally refunded, except where the law where you live says otherwise. If something is wrong, write to us anyway and we'll see what we can do.

Taxes and the seller

Gumroad is the seller of record and refunds the price and any tax it charged. The full purchase terms are in the Terms.
