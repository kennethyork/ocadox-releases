Quiet by design

Why your fans scream, and how Ocadox stops it

An agent keeps a model generating for minutes at a time, flat out, and the fans ramp up to match. Other local AI apps let the heat build. Ocadox paces the work and lowers the power while it generates, then puts everything back.

  1. The agent takes a step.It reads a file, writes code or runs a command.
  2. Ocadox checks the temperature.Of the graphics card, or the processor in CPU mode. Above your target (60 °C by default for a graphics card), it waits.
  3. It cools, then work continues.No admin rights, no driver tweaks.

Silent, Balanced or Turbo

Pick how hard it works. Silent paces the reply and eases off as the card nears 65 °C, Balanced at 75 °C; Turbo goes flat out when you're out of the room.

Power cap Optional

Lower the power only while a model generates: watts on NVIDIA cards, turbo boost or the power limit on the processor.

No graphics card? CPU mode

Office desktops and laptops run models on the processor. Ocadox notices, asks the model for less, and keeps the processor cool too.

Live thermal panel

Temperature, fan, power and load while you work, and a card after every run: how hot it got, how loud, how many watts.

Any GPU

NVIDIA, AMD and Intel graphics, on Windows and Linux. NVIDIA gets the most detail.

Night Shift Pro

Queue big jobs for quiet hours, and see the energy they used.

Your quiet week · free

See how quiet it stayed

Once a week, Ocadox adds it up: how long it worked for you, how much of that time the fans stayed quiet, the hottest it got, and the energy it used. One click turns it into a card to share.

01

A coding agent, and bots that keep going

The agent works in your project folder and asks before it runs anything. A bot has its own name and face, chat, folder and model: give it a job and close the window, and the result is waiting when you look again.

Works in your project

Reads, edits and searches files, runs commands with your OK, uses git, and browses the web only if you turn that on. Each chat stays in its folder.

Any model, in the app

From the Ollama library or any GGUF on Hugging Face, labelled by whether it fits your GPU. Already use LM Studio, llama.cpp or vLLM? Their models work too, still local.

Checks its own work

Runs your project's check (cargo check, tsc, go build, Python) before it says it's done, and tries your web app in a hidden browser, console errors and all.

Helpers, one at a time

Hands a question to a helper with a fresh, empty context, which keeps long tasks on track on local models, and the fans calm.

Rules, hooks, terminal

Follows AGENTS.md or OCADOX.md, runs After edit: hooks, and ocadox run "fix the failing tests" works without the window.

Talk, or type, for hours

Whisper turns your voice into text on your PC. Long chats are summarised as they grow, saved, and searchable.

A board for each project New

Trello-style cards with checklists and due dates. Hand a card to the agent with Do it, or let a bot work through a column.

Git, built in New

See your changes and diffs, commit with a message your local model writes, push, pull, switch branches, clone, and open GitHub pull requests.

Cloud when you want it New

Use your own Claude Code on your Claude plan, or your own Codex on your ChatGPT plan. Or Sign in with OpenRouter for Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok and 450+ more, or bring your own API key. Optional, off until you choose it, and clearly marked.

Bots

  • Templates: test watcher, code reviewer, dependency checker, weekly summary, folder tidier, or your own. Free
  • They take turns on the GPU, so several bots never make it roar. Free
  • Routines: "every weekday at 8:00, run the tests and tell me what broke". Ocadox starts with Windows and can wake the PC for them. Pro
  • Results come to you as a notification, and can be saved as files. Pro
02

Create, edit and record

Set each one up once in Settings, with no other app to install. The models run on your PC, and what you make is saved there.

Images

Type /image_create and what you want. Z-Image Turbo, about a minute per image on a good graphics card.

Video

/video_create a clip from a description, or press Animate under any image. Wan 2.2, on your graphics card, with the fan guard and power cap on.

Speak in your voice

Add a short recording of your voice, then /speak anything. Qwen3-TTS, on your PC. Only your own voice, or one you have permission for.

Longer videos New

Choose the length, from 2 seconds to 2 minutes, made as chained 5-second parts. One line per scene makes a storyboard. Saved as MP4.

Voice-over New

Pick any video and write a script, or have your local model draft it. Ocadox speaks it in your own saved voice and adds it to the video as an MP4, with captions if you want them.

Edit by asking New

Under any picture, say what to change ("make it night", "remove the person on the left"), cut the subject out with a see-through background, or upscale it 4x. FLUX.2 klein and Real-ESRGAN, on your PC.

Quick video edits New

Describe it ("vertical for TikTok, cut the first 2 seconds, text at the top") and your local model fills in the trim, shape, music and text. Saved as an MP4.

Record a website New

A smooth scroll through any page, desktop or phone sized, in a private browser. Add a voice-over and you have a demo video.

Long video to Shorts New

Drop in a podcast, talk or stream. Ocadox writes down what's said, your local model picks the moments that stand on their own, and each one comes out vertical with big captions and its title, ready for TikTok, Reels and Shorts. No Opus Clip subscription, nothing uploaded.

Make music New

Describe it ("calm lo-fi beat with soft piano") and get a song or background track, instrumental or with lyrics it writes or you give, up to 4 minutes, fast or in best quality. ACE-Step 1.5 on your PC: no copyright strikes, no subscription.

Dub into 10 languages New

Your model translates what's said, and it's spoken in your own saved voice right where the original was, with captions to match.

Meeting notes New

Record a meeting or lecture, or drop in a recording: a summary, decisions and action items, with the action items on your board.

Edit by transcript New

Like Descript, on your PC: cross out words to cut them, drop the ums and long pauses in one click, captions that follow the speech, and studio sound.

Overdub New

Said the wrong word? Type the right one in the transcript and it's re-spoken in your own saved voice, right where it belongs.

Record Ocadox itself New

Record the app's own window, or a demo while the agent works on a task, then edit it and add your voice-over. All on your PC.

On autopilot New

Bots can do all of it: "every Monday, record my site, make it vertical, voice it over and draft a post". Heavy steps ask first.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need Ollama?

Yes. Ollama runs the models. If you don't have it, Ocadox offers to install it on first launch (or get it from ollama.com). You then download and manage models from inside Ocadox.

Which GPUs work?

NVIDIA, AMD and Intel, on Windows and Linux. The fan guard reads the GPU temperature on all of them. NVIDIA cards (and AMD on Linux) also show fan, power and load in the live panel; AMD and Intel on Windows show temperature and fan speed. The optional power cap is for NVIDIA cards. No graphics card at all? CPU mode turns on by itself and keeps the processor quiet instead.

Can I use cloud models like Claude or GPT, or Ollama's?

Yes, if you want to. In Settings > Cloud models, press Sign in with OpenRouter and approve in your browser (no key to copy), then pick from 450+ models with their prices: Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek and more. Or add your own API key for OpenAI, Anthropic and others. Already pay for Claude or ChatGPT? Use your own Claude Code (Settings > Cloud models > Sign in to Claude Code) or your own Codex, on your plan; Ocadox never sees your sign-in. It's off until you do. Or sign in to Ollama and pull one of its cloud models. Either way they're marked CLOUD: when you use one, your messages and the files the agent reads go to that provider. Your own key bills your account. Ocadox tells you once before you use an Ollama cloud model, and Settings can hide them so everything stays on your computer.

Can bots keep working while I'm away?

Yes, while the PC is on. A bot keeps going with the window closed. With Pro, Ocadox starts with Windows in the tray and runs each bot's routines on schedule, waking the PC from sleep if you ask. For bots that work while your laptop is off, keep them on an always-on PC and check in from the laptop or the Android app.

Is connecting my phone or laptop safe?

You pair with a one-time code shown on the PC (a QR code for phones). The code carries the PC's identity, so your device only ever talks to that PC, and the connection is encrypted. A paired device can use the bots and read and start chats, nothing else: no files, settings or shell. You can remove it at any time. Away from home, Tailscale connects them without opening ports on your router.

Does it need admin rights?

No. The fan guard works by pausing the agent until things cool. Only the optional GPU power cap needs administrator rights, and Ocadox asks only if you turn it on. In CPU mode, Windows needs no admin rights at all; Linux asks for your password once.

Is there a Mac or iPhone version?

There's a Mac version for Apple Silicon (M1 and later, macOS 11+): the .dmg on the download page. Until it's signed for Mac, open it the first time with right-click > Open. On a Mac the GPU temperature and fan readings aren't available yet, so the fan guard has less to go on. There's no iPhone app; the companion app is on Android.
